Angular directives have 3 types:

  • Coomponent Directives: i.e Component
  • Structual Directives: i.e *ngIf *ngFor *switch etc...
  • Attribute Directives: i.e *ngStyle *ngClass

1. *ngIf & else directive example:

2024 is Leap Year

2025 is not Leap Year

2. *ngFor directive example:

  • {{user.userId}} - {{user.userName}} - {{user.userEmail}}

3. *ngSwitch directive example:

It's Monday

It's Tuesday

It's Wednesday

It's Thursday

It's Friday

It's Saturday

It's Sunday

It's Holiday
